A Farewell Letter

June 2026  ·  Kolkata

Dear Suvam Da,

Three years ago, you stepped into the corridors of Vidyasagar College — perhaps a little uncertain, perhaps a little wide-eyed, but always carrying that quiet fire within you. Today, as we gather to bid you farewell, those same corridors feel a little emptier, and a little more full of your legacy.

You have laughed in these classrooms, debated in these hallways, dreamed under these skies. You have turned pages of textbooks and pages of your own story, and somehow made both magnificent. Vidyasagar — the ocean of knowledge — would be proud.

The University of Calcutta has given you its credential, but you have given it something in return: proof that its students carry the torch of curiosity, integrity, and heart.

Whatever road you choose from here — know that every lesson learned, every friendship forged, every late night endured, was building the person you are today. And that person is extraordinary.

Go well. Go far. And come back and visit us sometime.
